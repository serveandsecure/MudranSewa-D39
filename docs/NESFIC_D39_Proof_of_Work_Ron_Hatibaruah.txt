1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & PROOF OF FUNCTIONAL MVP
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This Proof of Work document demonstrates that the proposed solution for NESFIC-D-39 ("Mudran-Sewa") is NOT a theoretical proposal or conceptual slide deck. It is a fully engineered, tested, containerized, and globally deployed production platform engineered specifically for the Directorate of Printing & Stationery, Government of Assam.

The platform provides end-to-end digital provenance, raw paper reel and ream inventory management, inter-departmental e-requisitions across all 35 Assam districts, printing press machine telemetry (with paper trimming loss percentage monitoring), serialized publication stamping for The Assam Gazette (অসম ৰাজপত্ৰ), and tamper-evident QR E-Gatepasses with live GPS highway transit tracking.
