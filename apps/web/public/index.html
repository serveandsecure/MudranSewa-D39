Challenge Context & Objective

The Directorate of Printing & Stationery (Government of Assam) manages huge volumes of printing paper (reams, reels, GSM grades), official government outputs (The Assam Gazette, budget books, land records, judicial stamps), and supplies across 35 Assam districts.
